Two turntables, two [Schiit Mani](https://www.schiit.com/) phono stages, one amp — and a cheap grey switch box that never quite belonged in the stack.

Tuurd Zero is the fix: a two-input line switcher built into a broken Modi 2 chassis so the selector matches the Manis it sits on.

![Tuurd Zero stacked on dual Schiit Manis between two turntables](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/in-system.jpg)

## Why

I run two decks into two Manis. That means two line-level outputs and only one path into the system, so something has to choose which deck is live.

The old switcher worked, but it was the wrong size, wrong finish, and wrong vibe next to the Manis — a temporary box that had overstayed.

![The old grey switcher on the Mani stack — functional, not a match](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/old-switcher.jpg)

When a silver Modi 2 died, the DAC was done but the case was still a perfect Schiit mini chassis. Same footprint as the Manis, same family of metalwork. Better to turn that carcass into the switcher the stack actually needed than leave a mismatched box on top forever.

## How

Empty the Modi. Keep the chassis, ditch the dead DAC guts.

![Empty Modi 2 chassis on the bench](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/empty-chassis.jpg)

Layout the new life as a passive A/B line switch: two stereo inputs, one stereo output, one front toggle. That is six RCA jacks on the rear and a single switch up front — nothing fancy, just a clean selector in the right box.

![Chassis, lid, and parts laid out for the rebuild](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/parts-layout.jpg)

Mark, drill, and finish the aluminum. The silver Modi shell was sanded and refinished — matte black lid over a red chassis — so the finished unit reads as part of the Mani stack instead of a leftover project box. The thin red line under the front edge is the chassis showing through.

![Casework in progress — cutouts, sanding, layout marks](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/casework.jpg)

Mount the gold RCAs with insulating washers, wire the toggle as a two-input / one-output switch, button the lid down.

![Finished Tuurd Zero — front toggle, black lid, red chassis](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/finished-front.jpg)

![Rear panel — six RCA jacks for two inputs and one output](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/finished-rear.jpg)

Drop it on the Manis between the decks. Flip the toggle for deck A or deck B. Same height language as the rest of the Schiit pile, no orphan grey box in the middle.

![Tuurd Zero in the dual-turntable stack](/assets/images/tuurd-zero/in-system.jpg)
