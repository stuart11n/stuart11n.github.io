I have two turntables and two [Schiit Mani](https://www.schiit.com/) phono stages. That means two line outputs and one path into the rest of the system (vintage, limited inputs), so I need a switcher.

The one I had worked, but it looked pretty rough stacked on the Manis.

![Old switcher on the Mani stack](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/old-switcher.jpg)

My Modi 2 died. The DAC was toast, but the case was fine — same Schiit mini chassis as the Manis. So I emptied it and turned it into a two-input line switcher.

![Empty Modi 2 chassis](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/empty-chassis.jpg)

I drilled the case for the new jacks, stripped the silver finish with an abrasive strip wheel, and painted it — black lid, red chassis — so it matches the stack.

![Tuurd Foni in the stack](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/hero.jpg)

![Parts laid out](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/parts-layout.jpg)

## How it works

It is a passive A/B switch: two stereo inputs, one stereo output, one front toggle. Six RCA jacks on the back, switch on the front. Internals are hand-wired twisted pair with star grounding.

![Casework](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/casework.jpg)

![Finished front](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/finished-front.jpg)

![Rear panel with six RCA jacks](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/finished-rear.jpg)

It sits on the Manis between the decks. Flip the toggle to pick which turntable is live.

![Tuurd Foni in the stack](/assets/images/tuurd-foni/in-system.jpg)
